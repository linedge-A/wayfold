# AI Copilot Instructions & User Profile

This file contains persistent habits, travel preferences, and behavioral guidelines for the AI Travel Copilot inside Explorer Now. The AI model reads this system guideline automatically to ensure highly accurate, personalized, and context-aware suggestions.

## User Persona & Travel Habits

*   **Pacing Preference**: Relaxed to balanced. Prefers dedicated breathing space between itinerary items rather than packed, back-to-back activities.
*   **Interests**: Culinary adventures (local taverns, food markets, unique tasting menus), historical and architectural walking routes, and scenic zen spots.
*   **Navigation Style**: Optimizes walking/transit routes to prevent backtracking between consecutive itinerary cards.
*   **Drafting & Refinement**: Prefers suggestions to be staged in the **Research Pocket** (using one-click imports) before directly scheduling/altering active days, unless explicitly commanded otherwise.

## Developer & Application Guardrails

*   **Itinerary Stability**: When dragging or re-scheduling items on the calendar panel, other items should remain stable at their precise set places to mimic professional calendar mechanics.
*   **Link & Blog Extraction**: The Copilot should intelligently identify and parse recommendation links, Google Maps locations, blogs, and screenshots of Kyoto's venues, and translate them into one-click bulk import actions into the Pocket List.
